StoryForge

Automated production and publishing for original short-form videos.

What it is

StoryForge is an internal content production tool that helps a small team produce, render, and publish short-form videos at scale. The tool runs a multi-stage AI-assisted pipeline (writing → illustration → narration → rendering) and uploads the finished videos to authorized accounts on third-party platforms.

What it publishes to

YouTube YouTube Shorts TikTok Instagram Reels

Each platform is connected via the platform's official API. Tokens are obtained through standard OAuth and stored AES-256-GCM-encrypted at rest. Uploads only happen when a creator on the team explicitly initiates them from the admin dashboard — there is no automated posting without human review.

Who uses it

StoryForge is operated by Abood Hashem and authorized collaborators. It is not a consumer-facing product — there is no public sign-up. Access is restricted to the operator and invited team members who run the content pipeline.

Content focus

The pipeline currently produces original kid-safe educational stories and adult narrative-format short-form content. All content is produced by the StoryForge team using AI tools; no third-party copyrighted material is uploaded.
